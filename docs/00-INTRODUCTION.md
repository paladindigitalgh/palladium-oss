# Palladium Documentation

# 00 -- Introduction

## Purpose

Introduce Palladium, define its audience, and establish the goals of the
platform.

## What is Palladium?

Palladium is an Operations Support System (OSS) built specifically for
fiber Internet Service Providers.

Unlike traditional OSS platforms that focus on inventory, monitoring, or
provisioning as separate systems, Palladium unifies the operational
lifecycle into a single workspace. Customer management, network
infrastructure, asset tracking, provisioning, diagnostics, and
operational workflows all exist within one cohesive platform.

Palladium is designed around the idea that operators should think about
outcomes---not vendor-specific commands or disconnected tools.

Whether replacing an ONU, investigating a customer outage, provisioning
a new service, or auditing network assets, operators should remain
inside a single application that provides the context needed to make
informed decisions and safely execute changes.

## Goals

-   Reduce operational complexity.
-   Eliminate unnecessary context switching.
-   Present operational information consistently.
-   Execute repeatable workflows safely.
-   Maintain complete operational history.
-   Provide a foundation that grows with an ISP.

## What Palladium Is Not

Palladium is not:

-   A billing platform
-   A CRM replacement
-   An accounting package
-   A GIS platform
-   A monitoring suite
-   A ticketing system

Future integrations may exist, but Palladium's responsibility is
operating the network---not owning every aspect of the business.

## Core Capabilities

Version 1 focuses on:

-   Customer Management
-   Network Management
-   Asset Management
-   Provisioning
-   Diagnostics
-   Operational Workflows

## Design Philosophy

-   Operations first
-   Search before navigation
-   Workspaces instead of pages
-   Workflows instead of buttons
-   Events instead of scattered logs
-   Business intent over vendor implementation
-   Simplicity over feature count

## Target Audience

-   Customer Support Representatives
-   Network Engineers
-   Field Technicians
-   NOC Personnel
-   System Administrators

## Version 1 Scope

### Included

-   Customer management
-   Service management
-   OLT management
-   ONU and router asset management
-   Workflow engine (including provisioning)
-   Diagnostics engine
-   Explorer (ad hoc query and reporting engine)
-   Authentication and RBAC

### Deferred

-   Decision engine
-   AI-assisted recommendations
-   Billing integrations
-   CRM integrations
-   Grafana integration
-   GIS integration
-   Mobile applications

## Guiding Principle

> **Palladium exists to help ISP operators make the right decision, then
> safely execute it.**
